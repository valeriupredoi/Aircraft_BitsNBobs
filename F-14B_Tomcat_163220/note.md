# F-14B 163220 — service history

- kit: Tamiya F-14B Tomcat 61129
- decals: Fightertown Decals 48083 Tomcats at War Part 1 A/B/D 

BuNo 163220, construction number 581, was a US Navy Grumman Tomcat that served with VF-143, the “Pukin’ Dogs”, and flew with the squadron during its 1990 European tour and its later deployments to Afghanistan and Iraq. It was originally designated F-14A+ before the Navy redesignated the type F-14B in 1991.

In June 1990, 163220 appeared at Boscombe Down, England, for the Battle of Britain 50th-anniversary airshow. It wore AG-111 markings with VF-143, which was operating from USS Dwight D. Eisenhower (CVN-69). The F-14A+ designation reflected the aircraft's upgraded F110 engines. The Navy changed the designation to F-14B in 1991, so both designations can be correct depending on the date being discussed.

In March 2002, VF-143 deployed aboard USS John F. Kennedy with Carrier Air Wing Seven (CVW-7) to support Operation Enduring Freedom. The deployment lasted until August 2002. The squadron flew missions over Afghanistan, using the Tomcat's targeting and navigation capabilities for precision strike and close air support. VF-143 next deployed aboard USS George Washington (CVN-73) in 2004, supporting Operation Iraqi Freedom. The squadron flew strike missions over Iraq before transitioning from the F-14 to the F/A-18E Super Hornet in 2005.

The aircraft's final chapter followed its operational service with VF-143: March 2005: departed NAS Oceana for Davis–Monthan AFB, Arizona, and June 2008: transferred to HVF West in Tucson for disposal and subsequently recorded as scrapped.
