## Bell UH-1D Huey at the Battle of Ia Drang Valley

- A Co., 229 AHB 63-8808 "Ancient Serpent 6" · Maj. Bruce Crandall (MoH), 1965 November Battle of Ia Drang
- A Co., 229 AHB 63-8775 · Capt. Ed Freeman (MoH), 1965 November Battle of Ia Drang

Unit explained:
- AHB = Assault Helicopter Battalion
- 1st Cavalry Division

In his oral-history interview, he explained that his distinctive rattlesnake-emblazoned cabin doors were transferred between aircraft whenever he changed Hueys. Consequently, identifying one particular manufacturer's serial number for his Ia Drang missions is problematic. There is circumstantial evidence for both 63-8808 and 63-8888.
