## SLAB's F-16C

The Viper he flew when he crashed was 4056 (JC-17). SLAB, as Tigers Team leader, flew 4061 in 2024, at the Nowy Targ Airshow in July 2024.

**F-16C at Nowy Targ Airshow**

| Detail | Identification |
|---|---|
| Polish serial | **4061** |
| USAF serial | 03-0061 |
| Construction number | JC-22 |
| Variant | F-16C Block 52+ |
| Unit | 6 ELT, 31 BLT |
| Pilot | Capt. Maciej "SLAB" Krakowian |
| Confirmed date | 6–7 July 2024 |

From the airshow description: F-16 Tiger Demo Team Poland from the 31st Tactical Air Base Poznań-Krzesiny, was established in 2015. The team is made up of a group of over 20 soldiers; pilots, safety observers, a commentator, technicians, a logistician and a flight safety officer. In Nowy Targ, the F-16 nb 4061 aircraft, was flown by pilot captain Maciej ‘SLAB’ Krakowian. The demonstration lasted 14 minutes and included sequences of high overload manoeuvres, at altitudes from 150m – 2,000m and speeds from 200km/h to 1,200km/h. The pilot used thermal traps, or so-called flares, several times. Many spectators from Nowy Targ specifically came just for this show.
