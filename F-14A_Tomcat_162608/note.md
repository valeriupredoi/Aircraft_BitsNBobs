## F-14A Tomcat BuNo 162608 "Tomcat Fastfac" Operation Allied Force 1999

**Decals: Fightertown Decals 48083 Tomcats at War 3 A/B/D:**
- Fastfac 101, VF-41 "Black Aces", aboard USS Theodore Roosevelt (CVN-71), with Carrier Air Wing Eight (AJ)
- Operation Noble Anvil (US designation of Allied Force), May-June 1999
- crew: Cdr J J Bauser callsign DOG, Cdr B G Gawne callsign DONGER

### BuNo 162608 History

The key unit was VF-41 aboard USS Theodore Roosevelt (CVN-71), with Carrier Air Wing Eight (AJ). The carrier had departed on 26 March 1999, originally heading towards the Persian Gulf, but was diverted to the Balkans. The Navy entered the air campaign on 6 April 1999. VF-41 and sister squadron VF-14 flew combat missions over Kosovo, with the first Navy strikes hitting targets around Pristina. The carrier subsequently continued to the Gulf for Operation Southern Watch. One particularly useful visual reference is the photograph reproduced in F-14 Tomcat: The Bombcat material: 162608 appears alongside another VF-41 Tomcat over the hilly terrain of Kosovo in April 1999. The caption specifically identifies both aircraft as having seen considerable action during Allied Force (`TOmcats_Balkans_2`).
